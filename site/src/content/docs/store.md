The software is free forever and always will be. Hardware made for it — starting with the dew heater ring — is sold at a small margin to cover fab runs and shipping. Buying it is never required: everything on the [parts list](/hardware/) is off-the-shelf, and the heater works with a hand-wound resistor ring too.

## Skylapse 12V dew heater ring

<span class="pill">coming soon</span>

A purpose-made 12V heater ring for the dome, switched by Skylapse itself from a BME280 dewpoint reading (or by hand from the Heater tab). Estimated **~$20**. Details when it is real and in hand — see the [Dew heater](/dew-heater/) page for how the software side works and how to wire your own heater today.

## Why two stores

Etsy is where most people already have an account; Tindie is the maker-hardware marketplace where Raspberry Pi people actually look for boards like this. Same product, same price, whichever you prefer.
