The software is free forever and always will be. Hardware made for it — starting with the dew heater ring — is sold at a small margin to cover fab runs and shipping. Buying it is never required: everything on the [parts list](/parts/) is off-the-shelf, and the heater works with a hand-wound resistor ring too.

## Skylapse 12V dew heater ring

<span class="pill">listing soon</span>

A purpose-made 12V heater ring for the dome, switched by Skylapse itself from a BME280 dewpoint reading (or by hand from the Heater tab). It is real, in hand, and running in the reference rig: 50 mm hole / 85 mm outer, ~31 Ω, ~4.2 W at 12 V, two solder pads and nothing else on the board. **~$20**. The [parts list](/parts/#dew-heater-ring-switch-sensors) has the specs and the verification run, and the [Build page](/build/#3-wire-the-heater-switch) has the wiring, screw by screw.

## Why two stores

Etsy is where most people already have an account; Tindie is the maker-hardware marketplace where Raspberry Pi people actually look for boards like this. Same product, same price, whichever you prefer.
