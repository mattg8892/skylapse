Every clip here was rendered by the camera itself at dawn, from the full-resolution frames of one night, with nothing done to it afterwards except shrinking it for the web. The first two nights on the new 12V rig, both sensors and the heater ring in place.

## 29 October 2026

The first night with no interruptions of any kind: every frame on the grid, no restarts, the heater regulating against the dome sensor all night.
