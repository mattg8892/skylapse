## What it does

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="No terminal. Ever." icon="rocket">
    Write a card, plug it in, set it up on your phone. No config files, no account, nothing in the cloud. If there is no Wi-Fi to join, the camera serves its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Built for Pi cameras" icon="puzzle">
    The HQ Camera / IMX477 above all — that is what Skylapse is developed and tested against. Boards the Pi cannot auto-detect can be declared from the setup screen. ZWO ASI USB cameras are supported second, best-effort.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full-res JPEG + DNG raw" icon="document">
    A full-resolution JPEG every frame, plus DNG raw on demand, on a schedule, or from a keeper button for when a meteor just went past.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-exposure that tracks the sky" icon="sun">
    Day, night and twilight profiles from sun altitude, plus a manual mode for star-tracker rigs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nights browser" icon="list-format">
    Scrub a whole night frame by frame, with a star-count chart to find the clear stretch. Download any frame as JPEG or DNG.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Focus assist" icon="magnifier">
    Live view with 1×–10× zoom into the full-resolution sensor image and a sharpness score to chase while you turn the ring.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dawn timelapses" icon="open-book">
    Rendered automatically every morning, with one-off length and quality overrides.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Survives your network" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Falls back to its own Wi-Fi if your network disappears, and an access-point mode you can switch on by hand. Phone alerts over ntfy when capture stops — and when it recovers.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Capture, the web UI, and networking are three separate services, so a problem in one cannot stop the others writing frames.

## Free forever

Skylapse has one hard rule: **no cost to the maintainer, no cost to the user, ever.** No native app (it is a PWA — no app-store fees to pass on), no cloud relay, no hosted service, no account. Everything runs on your Pi. Nothing in the project may create a recurring bill for anyone, and nothing may depend on a backend that could be shut down. MIT licensed, clean-room implementation.
